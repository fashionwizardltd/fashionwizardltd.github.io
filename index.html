About Fashion Wizard

One accountable partner between your brand and the factory floor.

Fashion Wizard Ltd. has operated as an apparel buying agent out of Dhaka since 2003, working between international brands and a vetted network of Bangladeshi knit, woven and sweater manufacturers.

We exist because sourcing directly from overseas factories is slow, opaque and risky for brands without a team on the ground. Fashion Wizard sits inside that gap — reviewing factories in person, following production line by line, and reporting back in plain terms, so our buyers never have to guess what's happening on their order.

Our main markets are the United States and Canada, and our approach has stayed the same since day one: respond quickly, price honestly, deliver on schedule, and treat every stitch as if it carried our name, not just yours.

  • Quick response to every inquiry
  • Competitive, transparent pricing
  • In-time delivery, tracked from PO to port
  • Quality checked at every stage of production
